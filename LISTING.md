## Description

> **Stop wondering where your money went. Start 2027 knowing exactly where it's going.**
>
> This all-in-one budget spreadsheet turns 10 minutes a week into total clarity about your money. Type in what you earn and spend, and the dashboard does the math: monthly totals, savings rate, running balance, and which categories are over budget.
>
> **WHAT'S INSIDE (7 tabs)**
> ✔ **Dashboard:** income vs. expenses chart, spending pie chart, savings rate, and a 12-month overview
> ✔ **Budget vs. actual:** 18 categories, overspending highlighted in red automatically
> ✔ **Transaction log:** 1,000 rows with a category dropdown; income and expense detected for you
> ✔ **Savings goals:** shows exactly how much to save each month to hit your deadline
> ✔ **Debt payoff planner:** your debt-free date, total interest, and how extra payments speed it up
> ✔ **Bill tracker:** tick off recurring bills month by month and never miss one
> ✔ **Setup page:** rename any category; everything updates instantly
>
> **WORKS WITH**
> Microsoft Excel (desktop and online, free), Google Sheets (free), Apple Numbers, LibreOffice.
>
> **WHY PEOPLE LOVE IT**
> • No subscriptions, no apps, no bank logins. Your data stays yours
> • Beginner friendly: white cells are for you, shaded cells do the work
> • Printable: every tab fits neatly on one landscape page
> • Reusable every year
>
